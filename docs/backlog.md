# Product Backlog — AI Label Reader for Accessibility

## Purpose
This backlog lists the initial features of the application in priority order. Priorities may change as the project develops.

## High Priority
- **P1 — Capture or upload a product label image:** Allow users to provide an image of a product label.
- **P2 — Extract text using OCR:** Recognize printed text from the uploaded image.
- **P3 — Read extracted text aloud:** Provide text-to-speech functionality for accessibility.
- **P4 — Handle errors:** Inform users when an image cannot be read or contains no detectable text.

## Medium Priority
- **P5 — Plain-language summary:** Provide a short, easier-to-understand summary of extracted information.
- **P6 — Audio controls:** Allow users to play, pause, and replay spoken text.
- **P7 — Accessible interface:** Use clear labels, readable text, and straightforward navigation.

## Low Priority
- **P8 — Adjustable speech speed:** Let users adjust how quickly the text is spoken.
- **P9 — Multiple language support:** Explore support for additional languages.
- **P10 — Scan history:** Consider allowing users to revisit previously scanned labels.

## Initial Assumptions and Limitations
- Users can provide a reasonably clear image of a product label.
- OCR accuracy may be affected by blurry images, poor lighting, or unusual fonts.
- The initial version may support a limited number of languages.
- The backlog is preliminary and will be updated as requirements evolve.
